Follow Through is a student project from a business course (BUSI 3630) at Memorial University of Newfoundland, built by a four-person student team. This test version is for testers who are 18 or older.

## What we collect
- **Your tester code** (we give it to you), your phone's **time zone**, and your **settings** (weekly goal, reminder times, what's hardest for you).
- **Your plans:** what you want to do, when, and for how long.
- **Your check-ins:** done, partly or not yet, what got in the way, and any note you choose to add.
- **The fixes** you're shown, and which ones you try.
- **Basic app use**, for example when you open the app, open a reminder or start a timer. These records never include anything you typed.
- **When you agreed** to this page and confirmed you're 18 or older.

## What we don't collect
Your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or health information. We don't ask about diagnoses. If something you type looks like you might be in crisis, the app shows support contacts and doesn't save that text.

## Where it's stored and who can see it
- It's stored with Supabase, a database service, in its **Canada (Central)** region.
- **Only our team can see it, under your tester code.** The list that links codes to names is kept separately by the team. It's never stored in the app or the database.
- Reminders are created on your phone, not sent from a server.
- If you install through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, and any feedback you send through TestFlight, with us.

## What we use it for
To run the app for you, and to learn whether it helps (for example, how many days people check in and which fixes help). Our course report only shares results as group totals or by code, never with names.

## What we never do
Sell your data, use it for ads, or share it with advertisers or data companies.

## Not medical care
Follow Through is a study-planning tool. It isn't counselling or medical care, and we can't respond to emergencies.
- In danger right now: call **911**.
- Thinking about suicide, or worried about someone: call or text **9-8-8**, 24/7.
- MUN students: Student Wellness and Counselling Centre, **709-864-8500**.
